Since **Maw3ed** is not just a clinic app, but a powerful, multi-tenant operational engine, the marketing must shift from "Booking Software" to "Business Operating System." 

The core value proposition is the **"Booking Triad"** (1:1, 1:Resource, 1:Many), which allows the software to scale across any industry.

# Part 1: Promotional Campaign Strategy

**Campaign Theme:** *Beyond the Booking.*
**Core Message:** Stop managing appointments; start managing your entire operation. Whether it’s a private session, a rented space, or a full class, Maw3ed synchronizes your staff, your resources, and your revenue in one seamless flow.

### Target Audience Segments
1. **Wellness & Beauty:** Salons, Spas, Private Clinics (The `exclusive` mode).
2. **Leisure & Sports:** Padel courts, Gaming lounges, Studios (The `rental` mode).
3. **Education & Fitness:** Yoga studios, Training centers, Workshops (The `capacity` mode).

### High-Converting Ad Angles
*   **Angle A (The Chaos Solver):** "Tired of juggling WhatsApp messages, spreadsheets, and payment disputes? Automate the journey from QR check-in to final invoice."
*   **Angle B (The Scalability Play):** "One platform, infinite uses. Whether you rent out a room or run a masterclass, Maw3ed adapts to your business model."
*   **Angle C (The Professionalism Edge):** "Give your clients a premium experience. Instant booking $\rightarrow$ Digital QR $\rightarrow$ Automated Invoicing."
