### Product Architecture - Sam-Harness

#### Overview
This architecture document outlines the key components and layers of a multi-purpose booking & ops SaaS solution, focusing on a high-converting promotional campaign. The campaign aims to attract customers and improve sales by emphasizing flexibility, scalability, and comprehensive features.

#### High-Concept Campaign

**Tagline:**
"Unlock the Power of Flexibility, Scale with Us!"

**Tagline (Arabic):**
"استكشف قوة التحفيز، وافتح معنا!"

**Key Features:**
- **Multi-Purpose Booking:** Cover all types of services like medical, clinics, salons, beauty, and sports.
- **Resource Rental:** Rent equipment and facilities for events and operations.
- **Batch Sessions:** Schedule and manage group fitness sessions, educational workshops, and other group activities.
- **Full Lifecycle Management:** Book, check-in, and manage all bookings from appointment scheduling to final invoice generation.
- **Staff-Branch Statuses:** Manage staff and branches to ensure smooth operations and customer satisfaction.
